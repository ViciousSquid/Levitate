# Texture credits

The wall, floor, coping and metal materials are photo-scanned textures from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), published under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).
Fetched at 1k by `game/tools/fetch_textures.py`.

| Levitate! material | Poly Haven texture | Authors |
|---|---|---|
| `wall_slate` | [Concrete](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete) | Rob Tuytel |
| `floor_slate` | [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02) | Rob Tuytel |
| `wall_sand` | [Sandstone Blocks 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/sandstone_blocks_05) | Rob Tuytel |
| `floor_sand` | [Gravelly Sand](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravelly_sand) | Dario Barresi |

| `floor_moss` | [Mossy Cobblestone](https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_cobblestone) | Sơn Nguyễn |
| `cap_stone` | [Concrete Wall 008](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_008) | Charlotte Baglioni, Dario Barresi |
| `trim_metal` | [Metal Plate 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02) | Rob Tuytel |

`game/tools/make_pads.py`.
